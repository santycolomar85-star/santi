```
00-05s  TEASER MISTERIOSO
        Imagen: huevo brillante en el fondo del Pacífico abriéndose
        Texto: "Hace una marea atrás..."

05-15s  ORIGEN
        Imagen: Ecuaman joven nadando en manglares Manabí
        Texto: "Nací en Manabí. Aguas limpias. Familia honesta."
        Voz en off: ninguna

15-25s  CONFLICTO
        Imagen: cajas falsas de "camarón ecuatoriano" en puerto extranjero
        Texto: "Un día vi cómo otros se hacían pasar por mí."
        SFX: música baja drásticamente

25-35s  DECISIÓN
        Imagen: Ecuaman llorando una lágrima cian
        Texto: "Y juré algo:"

35-45s  TRANSFORMACIÓN
        Imagen: Ecuaman agarrando los 3 cristales, capa apareciendo
        Texto: "Mientras yo nade en estas aguas, nadie deshonrará el
                nombre de mi tierra."

45-55s  REVELACIÓN
        Imagen: Ecuaman heroico completo con tridente alzado
        Texto: "Yo soy ECUAMAN."

55-60s  CTA
        Imagen: logo + URL
        Texto: "Súmate a La Marea. Link en bio. 🔱"
```

### Prompt principal Veo (cinematic underwater epic):

```
60-second cinematic underwater origin story video, vertical 9:16 format,
about ECUAMAN — a heroic Penaeus vannamei shrimp superhero of Pacific
Ocean. The video must transition through these moments smoothly:

(1) Mysterious glowing cyan shrimp egg cracking open in deep dark Pacific
waters, soft bioluminescence, 5 seconds.

(2) Adolescent shrimp character with golden orange body swimming through
Ecuadorian mangrove roots, sunbeams piercing the water, 10 seconds.

(3) Underwater port scene where the character discovers fake "Ecuadorian
shrimp" boxes, his expression turning sad, lighting shifts to cooler
tones, 10 seconds.

(4) Extreme close-up of his face with a single glowing cyan tear falling,
emotional moody lighting, 10 seconds.

(5) Descent into deep hydrothermal trenches, character grabbing three
glowing cyan crystals, plasma energy emanating, royal blue cape forming
around him, 10 seconds.

(6) Full hero reveal: ECUAMAN in heroic frontal pose with raised cyan
trident, royal blue cape billowing, electric blue belt with white ECUAMAN
text and gold E medallion, red-yellow ribbon tassels on antennae, deep
Pacific Ocean background with god rays, DreamWorks Pixar 3D quality,
10 seconds.

(7) Logo card with "ECUAMAN" text and "ecuaman.ecuanutrition.com" URL,
5 seconds.

Style: Pixar/DreamWorks 3D semi-realistic, cinematic underwater lighting,
vibrant colors, dramatic god rays, high production value. No dialogue,
text overlays only at specified moments.
```

### Edición en post-producción

- **Editor recomendado:** Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve / Final Cut
- **Color grading:** intensificar cyan en tridente, calentar tonos en
  origen, desaturar ligeramente en conflicto
- **Texto overlay:** fuente Montserrat Black, color blanco, fade in/out
- **Audio:** mix con manifesto musical de banda sonora del lanzamiento
  (versión 60s)
- **Hook visual:** los primeros 3 segundos deben enganchar (algoritmo
  TikTok premia retención inicial)

```
00-05s  HOOK
        Imagen: anciano (Don Galo) caminando por el muelle
        Voz en off (Don Galo): "Mi'jo, salte. No te como."

05-15s  CONTEXTO

        de tres años marinos."

15-30s  ENSEÑANZA
        Imagen: Don Galo y Ecuaman joven hablando junto al estero
        Voz en off (Don Galo): "El cobarde no siente miedo. Siente
        miedo y se rinde. El valiente siente miedo y igual hace lo

30-40s  EMOCIÓN
        Imagen: Don Galo atándole la borla a Ecuaman joven
        Voz en off (Ecuaman narrador adulto): "Esa noche cambió mi
        vida para siempre."

40-45s  CTA
        Texto en pantalla: "Episodio 01 completo. Link en bio."
```

### Prompt Veo (emotional intimate scene):

```
45-second emotional vertical 9:16 video featuring an old Ecuadorian
fisherman called Don Galo (75 years old, weathered hands, white shirt,
straw Toquilla hat, kind weathered face) interacting with an adolescent
heroic shrimp character ECUAMAN at a small mangrove shrimp farm in
Manabí Ecuador.

Scenes:
(1) Wide shot of Don Galo walking by the edge of a shrimp pond at golden
hour, weathered boots on muddy ground, 5 seconds.

(2) Underwater close-up of young ECUAMAN (smaller version, golden orange
body, no cape yet, no trident yet, antennae visible, big curious eyes)
peeking out of the water cautiously, 10 seconds.

(3) Don Galo sitting on edge of pond talking warmly to the young
character, removing his Toquilla hat, looking out to horizon with wisdom,
golden hour lighting, 15 seconds.

(4) Tight emotional shot of Don Galo gently tying a small red and yellow
ribbon tassel onto the young shrimp's antenna, both characters with tears
in their eyes, deeply emotional family moment, 10 seconds.

(5) Text card with "Episodio 01 — link en bio", 5 seconds.

Style: Pixar/Coco emotional warmth, cinematic, deep human connection,
warm color palette, no flashy effects, focus on faces and emotion.
```
